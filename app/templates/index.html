Buddy Ticket Tracker

This application has been developed as part of a Software Engineering and DevOps module, and is based on the real “ready for buddy” process used within the QA function at Covéa Insurance.

In practice, testers and developers raise work items that require a buddy review before they can move forward. How long it has been waiting and asking for it to be picked up can involve manual checks and spreadsheets. The Buddy Ticket Tracker aims to provide a simple central view of those items, who they are assigned to and their ageing.

This will support:

  • Centralised visibility of buddy tickets for the QA team.
  • Clear assignment of buddies and ownership.
  • Ageing indicators to help prioritise older work.

Use the navigation bar to access the ticket tracker and if applicable the admin area for managers only.
